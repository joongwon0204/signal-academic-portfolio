## 1. Define the category

Add a slug, heading, and label to `_data/content_groups.yml`.

## 2. Add Markdown content

Create a dated post and use the new slug as its first category.

## 3. Verify the archive

Build the site and confirm that the new group appears automatically on the Notes page.
