## Purpose of this page

This example shows how a project log can describe a prototype without becoming a full lab notebook.

## Recommended structure

- State the problem and intended user or workload.
- Describe the design decision that matters most.
- Link to code, artifacts, or a live demonstration when available.
- Separate measured evidence from interpretation.

Replace this page with a concise record of what you built and what a reader should learn from it.
