## Before the experiment

- State one question that the experiment can answer.
- Record the baseline and the reason it is comparable.
- Define the metric, units, and aggregation method.

## After the experiment

Save the configuration, raw results, environment, and one bounded conclusion. This example shows how a study note can preserve evidence without becoming a full paper.
