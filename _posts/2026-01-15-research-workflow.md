## Example workflow

1. Record the question before running an experiment.
2. Version the configuration with the code.
3. Keep raw results separate from derived figures.
4. Save the environment and exact command.

## Why it belongs in a portfolio

A short process note can show how your result was produced and make the work easier to inspect or reproduce.
