# Third-Party Notices

Signal Academic Portfolio was developed from a personal site that originally used the [Academic Pages](https://github.com/academicpages/academicpages.github.io) template. Academic Pages was derived from the [Minimal Mistakes](https://github.com/mmistakes/minimal-mistakes) Jekyll theme by Michael Rose and contributors. Those projects are distributed under the MIT License.

This repository retains portions of the upstream Jekyll structure, includes, and Sass sources. The upstream copyright notice and MIT terms are retained in `LICENSE`.

## Fonts

The Signal-specific pages load the following typefaces from public CDNs:

- Barlow Condensed, IBM Plex Mono, and Source Serif 4 via Google Fonts
- Pretendard via the jsDelivr-hosted Pretendard project

Review the respective font licenses before changing the delivery method or redistributing font files.
